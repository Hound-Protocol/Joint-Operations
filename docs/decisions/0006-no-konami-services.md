# 0006: No Konami online services
Status: Accepted · Date: 2026-10-21 · Deciders: project lead

## Decision
Joint Operations never connects to, imitates for others, or interferes with Konami's online services (FOB, MGO, accounts, matchmaking servers). It does not touch anti-cheat. Players connect through Steam or directly, and any matchmaking runs on our own infrastructure, used only by our players.

## Consequences
- The game's online features that depend on Konami servers stay vanilla and separate.
- Saves used by Joint Operations are kept apart from the player's normal saves, to protect their online progress.
