# 0014: Quiet development until 0.1
Status: Accepted · Date: 2026-10-22 · Deciders: project lead

## Context
The repository, website and Discord are reachable, but there is nothing to play yet. Announcing too early would bring attention before there is a presentable version and a solid foundation that people can build on ([ADR 0009](https://github.com/Hound-Protocol/hound-protocol-web/blob/main/docs/decisions/0009-departments-and-ranks.md)).

## Decision
1. **Joint Operations is not made actively public until version 0.1.** No announcements, launch posts, trailers or promotion on Reddit, forums, YouTube or social media before 0.1.
2. The repository, website and Discord stay online. People who find them are welcome, and nothing is hidden or secret: we work in the open, we just do not advertise.
3. **Recruiting before 0.1 is personal and quiet:** direct invitations and word of mouth for the Quality and Administration departments. The recruiting kit ([recruiting.md](../recruiting.md)) is prepared but not posted.
4. Contracts stay on the website's board and are not published as GitHub issues to the public until the lead decides (tools/publish_contracts.py stays in dry run).
5. Donations stay closed until 0.1 ([ADR 0010](0010-funding.md)).
6. Version 0.1 is the public launch: release, announcement, recruiting posts and contracts open together.

## Consequences
- Dev log and releases keep being published on the website and GitHub, without promotion.
- Anyone posting about the project before 0.1 should check with Administration first.
