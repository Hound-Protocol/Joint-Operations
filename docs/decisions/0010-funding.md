# 0010: Donations open at version 0.1
Status: Accepted · Date: 2026-10-22 · Deciders: project lead

## Context
Donations could help with hosting and development, but taking money before there is anything playable invites expectations the project can't meet yet. See [funding research](../../research/funding-and-distribution.md).

## Decision
- **No donations before version 0.1.**
- At 0.1, donations open through a fiscal host (such as Open Collective Europe), optionally linked to GitHub Sponsors.
- Money goes to hosting and development only. Every expense is published.
- Money never buys influence over decisions.

## Consequences
- The website shows no donate button until 0.1.
- Before 0.1 ships: choose the fiscal host, read its terms, and write the reporting plan in [legal.md](../legal.md).
- Contributor records carry no money value. Any payout rules are published separately once donations exist.
