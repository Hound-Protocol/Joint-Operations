# 0001: Multiplayer mod, not a co-op add-on
Status: Accepted · Date: 2026-10-21 · Deciders: project lead

## Context
The project was first described as co-op. The project lead clarified the goal: transfer the game's online functionality into the single-player world.

## Decision
Joint Operations is a **multiplayer mod**. It brings the online multiplayer functionality of MGSV into the single-player world, so up to 16 players share the open world and its missions, with seamless joining and leaving.

## Consequences
- All documents, the website and recruiting use "multiplayer mod" wording.
- Scope includes free roam, missions, Mother Base staff as player characters, and session join and leave, not just a second player in a mission.
- Strategy work (0002) favors reusing the engine's own online systems.
