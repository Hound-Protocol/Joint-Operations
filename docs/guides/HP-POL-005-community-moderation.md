# Community and Moderation Policy
Code: HP-POL-005 · Version 1.2 · Effective 2026-10-23 · Owner: Administration · Applies to: GitHub, Discord and every Hound Protocol space

<!-- hp:join-discord -->

## 1. Standards
The [Code of Conduct](../../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) and the Discord rules apply everywhere. In short: be respectful, stay on topic, no piracy, no cheating in others' games, no Konami online services, no harassment, hate or spam.

## 2. Enforcement ladder
| Step | When | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Note | Small, first-time issue | Friendly public or private reminder |
| 2. Warning | Repeated or clear breach | Written warning, logged in #mod-log |
| 3. Timeout | Continued after warning | Timeout of 1 hour to 7 days |
| 4. Removal | Serious or repeated breach | Kick or ban, access removed |
| Immediate removal | Piracy, game files, leaked secrets, harassment, threats, doxxing | Content removed and person banned at once |

## 3. How moderators act
Moderators are appointed once there is a playable prototype. Until then the project lead moderates alone; where this policy needs two moderators, the project lead decides and writes down why in #mod-log.
- Act on behavior, not opinions. Explain the action briefly.
- Log every warning, timeout and ban in #mod-log: who, what, why, link.
- Never moderate a dispute you are part of. Hand it to another moderator.
- Two moderators must agree on a permanent ban, except in the immediate-removal cases.
- A ban is an LV0 ban across every linked account (GitHub, Discord, Steam), carried out with [HP-SOP-012](HP-SOP-012-bans.md).

## 4. Appeals
Appeal to the Administration Department within 30 days. A moderator who was not involved reviews it. The project lead has the final say.

## 5. Recruiting and promotion
Follow the posting rules in the [recruiting kit](../recruiting.md): unofficial and early, no promises, no criticism of other mods, one post per venue.
