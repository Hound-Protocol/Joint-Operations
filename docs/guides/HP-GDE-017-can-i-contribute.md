# Can I Contribute?
Code: HP-GDE-017 · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-10-23 · Owner: Administration · For: anyone wondering whether they can help

**Short answer: almost certainly yes.** You do not need to be a programmer, an expert, or available every week. Hound Protocol is a passion project with no deadlines and no time pressure. What we ask for is care. This guide helps you find out where you fit, in about five minutes.

## 1. The only hard requirements
You can contribute if all of these are true:

| Requirement | Why |
|---|---|
| You can use a free **GitHub** account with two-factor authentication turned on | All work and credit lives there ([GitHub Basics](HP-GDE-014-github-basics.md), [Account Security](HP-GDE-010-account-security.md)) |
| You agree to the [Contributor Policy](HP-POL-001-contributor-policy.md) and the [Code of Conduct](../../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) | Everyone works by the same rules |
| You will not share game files or copy other mods' code ([HP-POL-003](HP-POL-003-clean-room-and-ip.md)) | It keeps the project legal and open |
| For testing or anything in the game: you own a **legal copy of MGSV: The Phantom Pain** on PC | We never share or touch pirated copies |

Everything else (skill, experience, time, language, age of your PC) only changes *what* you do, not *whether* you can.

## 2. Find your fit: answer five questions
Go down the list. Every "yes" is a way in.

| Question | If yes, you can… | Start here |
|---|---|---|
| Do you play MGSV on PC (Windows, or Linux with Proton)? | **Test** builds and tools, and report what breaks | Ask for the Windows Tester or Proton Tester role on Discord; [Testing guide](HP-GDE-004-quality-testing.md) |
| Do you notice mistakes, unclear sentences or broken links? | **Review** documents and pull requests, or fix typos | [Reporting a Problem](HP-GDE-016-reporting-a-problem.md), section 6 of [GitHub Basics](HP-GDE-014-github-basics.md) |
| Can you write clearly, or explain things to beginners? | Improve **guides and documentation** | [Documentation Style](HP-GDE-005-documentation-style.md) |
| Do you like digging into how things work, reading docs and forums? | Do **research**: sourced notes in your own words | [Writing Research Notes](HP-GDE-013-research-notes.md), [Phase 1 plan](../phase1-research-plan.md) |
| Can you program (C++, Lua, Python, JavaScript), or are you learning? | Take **contracts** once they open, from Easy to Hard | [Contributor Quick Start](HP-GDE-003-contributor-quick-start.md), the website's Contribute page |

Not on the list? Design (made by people, no AI art), community help, translation, organizing tests, and explaining the project to newcomers all count too. Say what you like doing on Discord and we will find the place.

## 3. What is open right now
Version 0.1 (the first playable build) is being built by the project lead. Until then:
- **Open now:** Windows testers, Proton testers, reviewers, and help with documents and research.
- **After 0.1:** contracts on the board open to everyone. You can already read them and pick the one you would like.
- **After the playable prototype:** staff seats (technical lead, moderators, Discord admins, release signers). See [Governance](../governance.md).

## 4. Common worries
| "But…" | The answer |
|---|---|
| "I am not good enough." | Contracts are marked Easy, Medium and Hard, and anyone can take any of them. Reviewers help you get it right; changes asked in review are normal, not a failure. |
| "I have never used GitHub." | Most people start that way. [GitHub Basics](HP-GDE-014-github-basics.md) takes you from no account to your first pull request without programming. |
| "I only have a few hours a month." | There are no deadlines. Take something small, or leave a contract with a short handoff note if life gets busy. Nobody is penalized for being slow. |
| "My English is not perfect." | Clear is enough. Reviewers will help with wording. |
| "I use AI tools." | Welcome, with the same checks as any work, and credited openly. See [Working with AI Assistants](HP-GDE-018-working-with-ai-assistants.md). AI art is the one thing we do not accept. |
| "My PC is old." | Documentation, research and review need no gaming PC. Testers with older hardware are useful too: they find performance problems. |
| "I am not sure I want to commit." | You do not have to. Contractors take one contract at a time and can leave whenever they like. |

## 5. Your first step, today
1. Join the [Discord](HP-GDE-008-discord-members.md) and say hello in #general: one line on what you enjoy and how much time you have.
2. Create a GitHub account with two-factor turned on, if you do not have one.
3. Pick one thing from section 2 and do the smallest version of it: a typo fix, a test report, or reading the research plan and asking one question.

That first small thing is how everyone here started. Your work is credited to you on the contributor wall and in signed records ([Grades and Hound IDs](HP-GDE-009-grades-records-hound-ids.md)).
