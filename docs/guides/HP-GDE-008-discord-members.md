# Discord Member Guide
Code: HP-GDE-008 · Version 1.1 · Effective 2026-10-23 · Owner: Administration · For: everyone on the Hound Protocol Discord

The Discord server is where the team talks, tests and plans. It is organized like a unit: departments with their own channels, keycard roles and contractor grades.

<!-- hp:join-discord -->

## Joining, step by step
1. **Get Discord.** Use the app (Windows, macOS, Linux, phone) from discord.com, or discord.com/app in a browser. On Bazzite, install **Discord** from the Bazaar or Flathub.
2. **Make an account** if you do not have one, and confirm your email. Discord asks for this before you can talk on our server.
3. **Turn on two-factor authentication:** User Settings (the gear next to your name) → **My Account** → **Enable Two-Factor Auth**. Moderation channels require it, and it protects you ([HP-GDE-010](HP-GDE-010-account-security.md)).
4. **Open the invite:** **https://discord.gg/QTpJ89kMCM** (also on the website's main menu and in the README). Choose **Accept Invite**.
5. **Onboarding:** Discord shows a short setup. Pick what you are here for: **Windows Tester** or **Proton Tester** opens the QUALITY channels; you can skip it and pick later under **Channels & Roles** at the top of the channel list.
6. **Read #rules and #welcome.** By staying, you agree to the [Code of Conduct](https://github.com/Hound-Protocol/hound-protocol-web/blob/main/docs/code-of-conduct.md).
7. You now hold **LV2 · Member**, which opens the community channels. Say hello in **#general**.

**Tips**
- **Notifications:** right-click the server icon → **Notification Settings** → **Only @mentions**, then follow **#announcements** if you want release news.
- **Nickname:** right-click the server icon → **Edit Server Profile** to use the same name as on GitHub, so people recognize you on both.
- **Safety:** nobody from Hound Protocol will ever ask for your password, a login code, a token or payment. Report anyone who does to a moderator ([HP-POL-004](HP-POL-004-security-policy.md)).

## Roles
- **Keycards** (LV2 to LV7) show your access level. They match the website and GitHub ([ADR 0011](../decisions/0011-keycard-access.md)).
- **Rank E to S++** roles show a contractor's overall grade, their best grade in any field ([Contractor Grades Guide](HP-GDE-009-grades-records-hound-ids.md)). They are cosmetic and give no authority.
- **Unit roles** (for example RE / Hooks) show what someone works on.

How to get them:
- **Tester:** say hello in #test-sessions and tell us your system. Administration gives you access.
- **Contractor:** complete a contract (contracts open after 0.1). A records signer gives you **LV3 · Contractor** and your grade role.
- **Department member:** by invitation from Administration ([HP-SOP-007](HP-SOP-007-onboarding-offboarding.md)). Staff seats such as moderator and Discord admin are filled once there is a playable prototype.

## Who decides what
Moderators and Administration decide on conduct. Development decides technical matters. Anyone can talk to anyone, and you can appeal a decision one level up ([HP-POL-007](HP-POL-007-chain-of-command.md)).

## Rules in short
- Be respectful. No harassment, hate or spam.
- Own your copy of MGSV. No piracy, cracks or game files.
- Nothing that touches Konami's online services or anti-cheat.
- Never share passwords, tokens or keys, not even in DMs to team members.
- Before version 0.1, please do not promote the project elsewhere ([ADR 0014](../decisions/0014-quiet-until-0-1.md)).

## Discord and the website
- Your keycard level on Discord matches your keycard on the website. Sign in on the website with GitHub to see it ([Using the Website](HP-GDE-015-using-the-website.md)).
- To prove to someone on Discord that you hold a Hound ID, use **Verify → Prove a Hound ID** on the website ([HP-GDE-009](HP-GDE-009-grades-records-hound-ids.md)).
