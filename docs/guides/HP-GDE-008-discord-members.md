# Discord Member Guide
Code: HP-GDE-008 · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-10-22 · Owner: Administration · For: everyone on the Hound Protocol Discord

The Discord server is where the team talks, tests and plans. It is organized like Mother Base: departments with their own channels, keycard roles and contractor ranks.

## Joining
1. Join with the invite on the website or in the README.
2. Read **#rules** and **#welcome**. By staying, you agree to the [Code of Conduct](../../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
3. You get **LV2 · Member**. That opens the community channels.

## Roles
- **Keycards** (LV2 to LV7) show your access level. They match the website and GitHub ([ADR 0011](../decisions/0011-keycard-access.md)).
- **Rank E to S++** shows a contractor's best grade ([Contractor Grades Guide](HP-GDE-009-grades-records-hound-ids.md)). Ranks are cosmetic and give no authority.
- **Unit roles** (for example RE / Hooks) show what someone works on.

How to get them:
- **Tester:** say hello in #test-sessions and tell us your system. Administration gives you access.
- **Contractor:** complete a contract. A records signer gives you **LV3 · Contractor** and your Rank role.
- **Department member:** by invitation from Administration ([HP-SOP-007](HP-SOP-007-onboarding-offboarding.md)).

## Who decides what
Moderators and Administration decide on conduct. Development decides technical matters. Anyone can talk to anyone, and you can appeal a decision one level up ([HP-POL-007](HP-POL-007-chain-of-command.md)).

## Rules in short
- Be respectful. No harassment, hate or spam.
- Own your copy of MGSV. No piracy, cracks or game files.
- Nothing that touches Konami's online services or anti-cheat.
- Never share passwords, tokens or keys, not even in DMs to team members.
- Before version 0.1, please do not promote the project elsewhere ([ADR 0014](../decisions/0014-quiet-until-0-1.md)).
