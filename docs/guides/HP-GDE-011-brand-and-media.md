# Brand and Media Guide
Code: HP-GDE-011 · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-10-22 · Owner: Administration · For: everyone who makes graphics, posts or videos about the project

How to use the Hound Protocol and Joint Operations names, logos and colors. The full reference is the [brand guide](https://github.com/Hound-Protocol/hound-protocol-web/blob/main/docs/brand.md).

## The two brands
| Brand | Colors | Mark |
|---|---|---|
| **Joint Operations** (the mod) | Orange and white | The emblem: three operatives in a ring of sixteen marks. Small sizes use the sixteen-mark diamond |
| **Hound Protocol** (the team) | White, black and red | The hound in a black hexagon, with a red ring, eye and collar |

Files are in `joint-operations-web/site/assets/brand/`: full color, one color (`-mono`), light backgrounds (`-light`), lockups with the name, and 512 px avatars.

## Do
- Use the files as they are. Keep clear space around a mark equal to a quarter of its width.
- On photos or busy backgrounds use the one-color files.
- Keep each brand in its own colors.
- Credit sources for anything you did not make yourself.

## Do not
- Stretch, rotate, recolor, add effects to or redraw the marks.
- Use Konami's logos, fonts, artwork, screenshots of their logos, or anything that could look official ([HP-POL-003](HP-POL-003-clean-room-and-ip.md)).
- Use **generative AI** images, art, music or voices ([HP-POL-002](HP-POL-002-ai-use-policy.md)). AI may operate tools, such as layout in Canva, but the artwork must be made by people or drawn as vectors from our own designs.
- Put third-party stock graphics into a logo. Stock licenses (Shutterstock, Vecteezy and others) do not allow logo use without a paid license, and some forbid sharing their source files in our public repository.

## Third-party graphics
If you use a free or bought graphic somewhere else, such as a website background:
1. Check its license: is it allowed for websites, is credit required, and is it marked as AI-generated?
2. Commit only the finished image, never the source file, if the license forbids redistribution.
3. Add the credit to `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` and wherever the license asks.

## Posting about the project
Before version 0.1, we do not promote Joint Operations publicly ([ADR 0014](../decisions/0014-quiet-until-0-1.md)). Ask Administration first. From 0.1, use the link banner (`joint-operations-web/site/assets/img/og.png`) and always say it is an unofficial fan project, not affiliated with Konami.
