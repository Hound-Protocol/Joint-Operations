# How to Contribute
Code: HP-GDE-019 · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-10-23 · Owner: Development · For: everyone who wants to help

These are the contribution guidelines: how contributing works from the first look to the signed record, and the rules everyone follows.

**Not sure you can contribute?** Start with [Can I Contribute?](HP-GDE-017-can-i-contribute.md). Almost everyone can, and it shows you where you fit in five questions.

## 1. What you can do now
- **Before 0.1:** version 0.1 is being built by the project lead. Testers, reviewers, and help with documents and research are welcome now; ask on the [Discord](HP-GDE-008-discord-members.md).
- **After 0.1:** contracts on the board open to everyone. You can already read them on the website's **Contribute** page.

## 2. Get ready (once)
1. A GitHub account with two-factor turned on ([GitHub Basics](HP-GDE-014-github-basics.md), [Account Security](HP-GDE-010-account-security.md)).
2. Read the [Contributor Policy](HP-POL-001-contributor-policy.md) and the [Clean-Room and IP Policy](HP-POL-003-clean-room-and-ip.md).
3. Set up your tools with the [Contributor Quick Start](HP-GDE-003-contributor-quick-start.md).
4. Sign in on the website: your Profile shows your keycard and what your clearance opens ([Using the Website](HP-GDE-015-using-the-website.md)).

## 3. Taking a contract
1. **Pick** an open contract on the Contribute page. Difficulty (Easy, Medium, Hard) tells you what to expect; it is not a gate. An Easy one is a good start.
2. **Claim** it on the website: sign in, then **Claim** on the card. GitHub vouches for your account, and you appear in the card's **Contributors** list at once. **Unclaim** leaves it. On GitHub, commenting `/claim` on the issue does the same. Several people can claim the same contract; say hello and split the work.
3. **Work** on a branch in your fork. Sign off every commit (`git commit -s`).
4. **Open a pull request** that says `Closes #number`, and fill in the template, including whether you used AI tools ([Working with AI Assistants](HP-GDE-018-working-with-ai-assistants.md)).
5. **Review:** Quality checks it against the acceptance criteria and merges it. Changes asked in review are normal.
6. **Record:** you get a signed record, and the contract counts toward your grade in its field ([Grades, Records and Hound IDs](HP-GDE-009-grades-records-hound-ids.md)).

The full lifecycle is in [HP-SOP-001](HP-SOP-001-contract-lifecycle.md), and review in [HP-SOP-002](HP-SOP-002-pull-request-review.md).

## 4. Everything goes on the board
All work appears on the Contribute page, whoever does it, the owner and AI-assisted work included. Each card shows who is working on it and, once finished, who completed it ([HP-SOP-003](HP-SOP-003-release.md)).

## 5. The rules
| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| Pull requests only. Nobody pushes to `main` | Every change is reviewed and checked |
| No game files, no code copied from other mods | The project stays legal and open ([HP-POL-003](HP-POL-003-clean-room-and-ip.md)) |
| AI tools are welcome for code and documents; no generative AI art, music or voices | Credit the tool; art comes from people ([HP-POL-002](HP-POL-002-ai-use-policy.md)) |
| Nothing touches Konami's servers or anti-cheat | Offline play and our own sessions only |
| Leaving is fine; post a short handoff note first | Someone else can pick it up |
| Be kind; disagree with the work, never the person | [Code of Conduct](../../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) |

## 6. What you get
- **Credit:** your name on the contributor wall and in signed records, with any AI tools you used named alongside.
- **Grades:** E to S++ in each field, from completed contracts. Grades never go down and give no authority over others.
- **Clearance:** a completed contract earns the **LV3** contractor card; higher cards come with a department role.

## 7. When something goes wrong
- Stuck or unsure: ask on the issue or on Discord. Nobody is penalized for being slow or for asking.
- Careless work and skipped duties can earn infractions, at every level; appeals are always open ([HP-POL-009](HP-POL-009-accountability.md)).
