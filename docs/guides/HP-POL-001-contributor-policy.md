# Contributor Policy
Code: HP-POL-001 · Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-10-22 · Owner: Administration · Applies to: everyone who contributes

## 1. Purpose
This policy sets the rules every contributor accepts when they work on MGSV: Joint Operations, whatever their department or rank. It protects contributors, players and the project.

## 2. Who can contribute
- Anyone who accepts this policy, the [Code of Conduct](../../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) and the [AI Use Policy](HP-POL-002-ai-use-policy.md).
- You need your own legal copy of Metal Gear Solid V for any work that touches the game.
- Before 0.1, development is done by the project lead ([ADR 0009](../decisions/0009-departments-and-ranks.md)). Quality and Administration roles are open now. Contracts open after 0.1.

## 3. Your contributions
1. **Sign off every commit** with `git commit -s`. Your sign-off certifies that you wrote the work or have the right to submit it, and that you license it under the project license.
2. You keep the copyright in your work. The project may use, change and redistribute it under the project license.
3. You are credited in the commit history, on the contributor wall and in your signed records.
4. Work that does not meet its acceptance criteria may be sent back or closed. That is not a judgment of you.

## 4. What you must never do
- Commit game files, extracted assets, or anything derived from them that reveals their content.
- Copy code from other mods without a license that allows it. Infinite Heaven's code and other unlicensed mods' code are off limits. See [HP-POL-003](HP-POL-003-clean-room-and-ip.md).
- Touch Konami's online services, FOBs, anti-cheat, or other players' online sessions.
- Share tokens, passwords, keys or other people's personal data, in any channel.
- Present someone else's work, or AI output you have not reviewed, as checked work.

## 5. Conduct
- Be respectful and specific. Criticize work, not people.
- Keep discussion in the right channel. Decisions go into pull requests and decision records, not private messages.
- Disagreements go to the module owner, then to the project lead, who decides.

## 6. Leaving
You can leave at any time. If you hold a contract, post a short handoff note on the issue first (see [HP-SOP-001](HP-SOP-001-contract-lifecycle.md)). Access is removed the same day you leave a seat or department ([HP-SOP-007](HP-SOP-007-onboarding-offboarding.md)).

## 7. Breaches
Breaches are handled under the [Community and Moderation Policy](HP-POL-005-community-moderation.md). Committing game files, leaked secrets or copied code is removed at once and may lead to removal from the project.
